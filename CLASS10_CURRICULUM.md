# Class 10 chapter and topic catalogue

NCERT books, reprint 2026–27; CBSE 2026–27. Checked 10 October 2026. Original app-sized learning units, not copied textbook passages or a verbatim official index. This catalogue is a planning artifact; the running app still has its existing seven-topic pilot.

Each topic will follow **AI understanding → exam notes → relevant activity → recall quiz**. Written practice exams remain available independently. Teaching language: English/Hindi/Hinglish. Exam answers: English/Hindi.

Official contents: [Maths](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/jemh1ps.pdf), [Science](https://www.ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/jesc1ps.pdf). Assessment scope: [CBSE Maths](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/SecPart1/Maths_SecP1X_2026-27.pdf), [CBSE Science](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/SecPart1/Science_SecP1_2026-27.pdf).

## Mapping and publication status

Topic boundaries and activity choices are editorial proposals. Chapter numbers follow the current NCERT books. Our Environment is grouped with Biology. Core chapter coverage does not imply every textbook item belongs in the year-end exam.

Official chapter PDFs were accessible for 22 of the 27 chapters. Fetches failed for Science 4, 7, 8, 12 and Maths 9; their chapter titles were verified in official contents and broad coverage checked against CBSE. Their detailed topic splits require a direct textbook/teacher check. All topics need exact section/page mapping, content review and assessment tagging before publication.

CBSE distinguishes supplementary formative material from year-end content. Its Science teacher note is broader than some preceding content entries for heredity/magnetic effects; resolve that wording before advertising board-exam coverage. Do not silently remove these chapters or classify the entire chapters as formative-only.
